TSNARI Music Bot - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

1. Information We May Process

TSNA Music Bot may process basic Discord information required to operate its features, such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, usernames, command usage, and bot configuration settings.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide bot functionality, maintain server settings, troubleshoot issues, prevent abuse, and improve the bot.

3. Data Sharing

We do not intentionally sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services required for the bot's operation.

4. Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the bot's functionality or maintain required configuration.

5. Data Removal

If you want information associated with your Discord account or server removed, contact the TSNARI Music Bot owner or support team through the official Discord server.

6. Children's Privacy

TSNARI Music Bot is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the TSNARI Music Bot owner or support team through the official Discord server.
